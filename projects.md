# Strategic Product Deep Dives & AI Work

## Project 1: Agent Certification Framework (ACF)
- **Role:** End-to-End Product Builder (Strategic Concept through Production Code)
- **Tech Stack:** Multi-tenant web framework architectures, automated testing suites, federated SSO engines, configurable decision engines.
- **Core Problem:** The absolute lack of concrete, governance-backed mechanisms to evaluate whether autonomous AI agents are enterprise-ready, safe, and legally/operationally viable before and after deployment.
- **Solution & Architecture:** Conceived and deployed a governance product mapping out assessment journeys and maker-reviewer-approver workflows. The system maps business value against cost, risk, and compliance metrics, shifting from simple pre-creation GO/NO-GO rules to continuous post-deployment lifecycle guardrails and remediation tracks.

## Project 2: AI Core Financial Orchestration Platform
- **Role:** Principal Architect / Solo Product Builder (Active Development)
- **Tech Stack:** Multi-bank API networks, real-time algorithmic reconciliation, FX automated decisioning systems, AP/AR logical workflows.
- **Core Problem:** Mid-to-large scale organisations suffer from highly fragmented cash visibility across isolated global bank networks, high currency exposure fees, manual payment exception management, and sub-optimal routing across payment rails.
- **Solution & Action Items Built:** Architected a unified financial ledger orchestration engine that executes four specific operational action items:
  1. **Real-time Unified Liquidity View:** Consolidates balances dynamically from multiple disparate global banking systems onto a single cash-visibility pane.
  2. **Algorithmic FX Rate Optimisation:** Built automated logic to scan and choose optimal FX execution paths to eliminate wealth degradation during international treasury transfers.
  3. **Dynamic Payment Rail Routing:** Programmed automated exception routing rules to dynamically route payments based on cost, settlement speed, and rail health.
  4. **Strict Financial Controls:** Engineered a secure human-in-the-loop mechanism alongside automated auditing trails to maintain absolute corporate compliance.
