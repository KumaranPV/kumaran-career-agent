## Project 1 — ACF: Agent Certification Framework

### Status
Working multi-tenant product; actively evolving.

### Kumaran's role
**Product creator / product owner / zero-to-one builder**

### Problem
Enterprises need a repeatable way to determine whether proposed AI agents are:
- economically viable
- appropriately governed
- operationally ready
- sufficiently controlled
- suitable for implementation

### Product thesis
Convert AI-agent governance from an advisory checklist into a repeatable enterprise product.

### Product capabilities developed
- structured assessment journeys
- business viability / economics / risk evaluation
- configurable decisioning
- maker-reviewer-approver governance
- organisational hierarchy
- multi-tenant isolation
- federated SSO / OAuth
- decision history
- automated testing
- production deployment

### Product evolution
Initial question:
> **Should this agent be built?**

Evolving question:
> **What needs to change to make this agent viable, and does it remain viable after deployment?**

## Project 2 — AI Core Financial Orchestration Platform

### Status
**Product concept / architecture and operating-model design**
Do not represent as a fully deployed production platform unless additional evidence is added.

### Kumaran's role
**Product concept owner / solution shaper**

### Problem
Mid-to-large organisations often have:
- fragmented cash visibility across multiple banks
- avoidable FX leakage
- manual payment exception management
- fragmented AP / AR workflows
- sub-optimal payment-rail selection
- reconciliation friction

### Product concept
A financial-orchestration layer combining:
- multi-bank balance visibility
- AP / AR context
- FX decision support
- payment-rail selection
- reconciliation
- sanctions / control workflows
- human-in-the-loop decision points

### Conceptual capabilities

#### Unified liquidity view
Consolidate balance and liquidity information from multiple banking relationships.

#### FX decision support
Evaluate FX options and surface execution recommendations based on cost and context.

#### Payment-rail selection
Recommend or route payment execution based on cost, settlement speed, geography, constraints and rail health.

#### Reconciliation and exception handling
Use workflow and agent support to identify and resolve mismatches.

#### Human-in-the-loop controls
Preserve explicit approval points for high-impact or regulated financial actions.

### What this project demonstrates
- product thinking in financial infrastructure
- treasury / payments domain integration
- agentic workflow design
- financial-control thinking
- operating-model design

### Boundaries
Current dossier evidence supports this as a **product concept / design direction**, not as proof of a fully deployed production system.

Evidence:
- `PROJ-FINORCH-001`
