# JD Fit Scoring Rules

## Required output

### Overall Fit
**XX% — Strong / Moderate / Weak**

### Confidence
**High / Medium / Low**

### Full Matches
Only where direct evidence exists.

### Partial Matches
Use when evidence is adjacent or incomplete.

### Gaps / Risks
Use for missing evidence, practical constraints, language, geography or work authorisation.

## Default weighting

| Dimension | Weight |
|---|---:|
| Domain / industry | 20 |
| Product / functional mandate | 20 |
| Leadership / scale | 15 |
| Technology / platform | 15 |
| Commercial / P&L | 10 |
| Transformation / execution | 10 |
| Geography / regulatory context | 5 |
| Work authorisation / practical fit | 5 |

Weights may be adjusted to match the JD.

## Scoring discipline
- Full match = direct evidence
- Partial match = adjacent evidence
- No evidence = do not award full score
- Do not increase score simply because the candidate is senior
- Work-authorisation friction should be reflected but should not erase capability fit

## Recommendation labels
- 90–100: **Strongly worth interviewing**
- 80–89: **Worth interviewing**
- 70–79: **Conditional / explore**
- 60–69: **Low priority**
- <60: **Not recommended**

These bands are guides, not substitutes for judgement.
